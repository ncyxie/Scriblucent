Maya Chen leads the seed hydration protocol at Harbor Laboratory. The protocol defines acute dryness as a rapid loss of water and minerals. Each test tray receives 500 milliliters of water every 8 hours during the first day. The amount falls to 250 milliliters for trays with reduced drainage.

Technicians record soil temperature every 4 hours and stop treatment if spreading mold appears. A follow-up assessment occurs after 14 days. The assessment compares water intake, leaf curling, and soil pressure with the tray's initial measurements.
