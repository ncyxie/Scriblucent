In 2016 he ran as an independent candidate in the election against Evelyn Hart.
In Southland he ordered a rescue mission into Costavia to evacuate embassy staff.
Arden introduced travel rules affecting seven neighboring countries, expanded the Riverland-Northland border checkpoint network, and implemented a family processing policy.
He later appointed three constitutional court judges.
He withdrew the republic from agreements on climate and trade.
In May 2024 he became the first former head of state in the fictional republic convicted under its campaign-finance law.
