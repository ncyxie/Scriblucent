- DHCP assigns temporary network addresses and keeps each lease for 24 hours.
- DNS translates domain names into IP addresses and caches successful responses.
- TLS verifies the server certificate before encrypted application data is exchanged.
- The backup service runs every 6 hours and retains 14 copies.
- During an outage, the technician reviews gateway logs and isolates the failing switch.
- The team rolls back the deployment if median latency exceeds 200 milliseconds.
