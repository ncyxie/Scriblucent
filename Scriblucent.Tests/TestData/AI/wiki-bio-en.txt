Helen Park is a British epidemiologist. She was born in 1971 in Leeds. Park studied medicine at the University of Edinburgh from 1989 and graduated in 1995. In 1998 she joined the National Health Laboratory as a field researcher. She published a study of urban water quality in 2001.

Park moved to Nairobi in 2004 to lead a regional vaccination program. The program reached 12 districts by 2007 and trained 80 local nurses. In 2010 she returned to Edinburgh as a senior lecturer. Her group released an open outbreak-tracking protocol in 2013. Schools could run one drill for less than thirty pounds.

The World Health Forum appointed Park to a standards panel in 2016. She helped write guidance for rural clinic supply chains. The guidance requires weekly stock checks and annual audits. In 2021 she received the Leeds Civic Medal for combining research with local training. Park continues to teach, advise the vaccination network, and study affordable diagnostic kits.
