Beta.4 makes it easier to reuse material while editing, with a consistent Duplicate action for flashcards, quiz questions, and mind-map branches. It also polishes dashboard layouts and everyday navigation.

### Build on what you already have

- **Duplicate without entering multiselect.** Right-click a flashcard, quiz question, or mind-map node and choose Duplicate, or use **Ctrl+D** while the item is focused outside a text field. Bulk duplication follows the same rules.
- **Keep the content, start fresh.** Flashcard copies retain their text, media, hints, tags, and linked notes, with fresh study progress. Quiz copies retain their questions, options, hints, and explanations without copying the previous answer result. Copies appear beside their originals.
- **Reuse a whole branch.** Duplicate a mind-map branch with its descendants, styles, and links. In manual layouts, copies are placed in free space; undo and redo let you reverse or restore the operation.

### Other changes

- Dashboard calendars fit narrow layouts more reliably when the sidebar changes the available space.
- Flagged quiz questions use a compact blue-purple review marker that stays distinct when the question is also selected or answered.
- Additional Lithuanian translations for action labels and AI model descriptions.
- Recent cards fade softly at overflowing edges, following smooth scrolling and clearing when every card fits. Both themes use the same treatment across all four dashboards.

Beta.3 brings editable drawings, more control over spelling, optional update notifications, and a more complete Windows setup. It also refines the cards, motion, and editing details introduced in beta.2.

### A drawing workspace you can return to

- **Sketch with more than a pen.** Use a marker, eraser, lines, arrows, rectangles, and ellipses. Hold Shift to constrain shapes, zoom in for detail, or pan around the canvas.
- **Keep editing after insertion.** Reopen a sketch from your note and continue working with its original strokes. Paint over an ordinary picture while keeping its original image protected underneath.
- **Undo the action you meant to undo.** Drawing, erasing, clearing marks, and changing the background support undo and redo. Drawings stay editable when saved and shared in Scriblucent's note format.

### Spelling with a way back

- **Manage your own dictionary.** Search, add, and remove custom words in Settings. Custom words apply across notes and languages, and changes refresh open editors.
- **Control what you ignore.** Review words ignored in the current note, remove individual entries, or check them all again. Ignoring lasts until that note closes; adding to the dictionary is permanent until you remove the word.
- **Reverse a choice where you made it.** Right-click an accepted word to stop ignoring it or remove it from your custom dictionary. Spelling marks now update more reliably across styled text, lists, rapid edits, and reopened notes.

### Know when an update is ready

Startup update checks notify you when a newer version is available. They are enabled by default and can be turned off in Settings. Read the full, formatted release notes inside the app and download the next setup when you are ready; updates are not installed automatically.

### A more complete Windows setup

- Install for all users under **Program Files**, with English and Lithuanian setup text and a light or dark appearance that follows Windows.
- Upgrade an existing installation without uninstalling first. Setup can replace an older per-user copy and recover from an interrupted upgrade, while keeping each account's library and downloaded models separate from the program files.
- Optionally associate Scriblucent files with the app. Opening an exported note, flashcard set, mind map, or quiz imports a library copy and opens its editor; the original file stays unchanged.

### Other changes

- More consistent flashcard editor and study layouts, with improved space for tags, hints, and Lithuanian labels.
- Improved note image previews and more reliable detection of pictures within the preview area.
- Reduced motion retains the core feel with fewer optional effects; Off shows dialog backdrops without a delayed entrance.
- Rounded scrolling viewports, coordinated editing accents, and clearer illustrated states in dark mode.
- Dashboard groups use sequential numbers instead of random identifiers.
- More stable note lists and checklists during editing, with better marker sizing and placement.
- Consistent modal backdrops across blocked windows and smoother, better-sized updater scrolling.
- Clearer Settings ordering, device-only logging descriptions, and more even sidebar spacing.
- Legacy data-conflict notices no longer repeat on every launch.
- Opening a written-answer quiz no longer falsely marks it as edited.
- Smaller installation payloads, remembered setup choices, and single-instance file opening.

### Four tools, one connected workspace

| Create | Make it useful |
| --- | --- |
| **Notes** | Write with rich text, images, checklists, tables, and sketches. Link notes, reuse templates, and return to earlier versions. |
| **Flashcards** | Build cards with text, images, and audio. Choose SM-2 or FSRS-6 spaced repetition, use Cram mode, and follow your progress. |
| **Mind maps** | Connect ideas, arrange branches, style nodes, and link them to your notes. |
| **Quizzes** | Mix choice, true/false, and written questions, then review attempts and missed answers. |

### An interface that feels alive

Light and dark themes, soft depth, cursor-responsive card tints, and coordinated motion give the dashboard and editors one visual language. Tabs keep your materials together, smooth caret movement makes writing more fluid, and reduced motion offers a quieter experience.

### Less friction in the daily work

- **Organize your way.** Group and tag materials, search your collection, and drag cards into a manual order.
- **Work in batches.** Select multiple items for common actions, including bulk renaming and export where supported.
- **Review across topics.** Combine flashcard sets or quizzes into a broader study set or test.
- **Keep practice in view.** Plan reminders in the dashboard calendar and choose the first day of your week.
- **Bring your materials along.** Import RTF and text notes, CSV or Anki plain-text flashcards, and GIFT or CSV quizzes. Export notes to PDF or selected materials together in a ZIP archive.
- **Make it yours.** Choose English or Lithuanian and adjust motion to your preference.

### Local first, AI when you want it

Write, organize, and study offline without an AI model. Optional local AI can turn notes into draft flashcards, mind maps, and quizzes. Models download when you choose them and run locally afterward. Review generated material before studying it.

### Ready for Windows

Setup installers are available for **x64** and **ARM64**, with the required runtimes included. Optional models download separately. This is a beta release; [report issues](https://github.com/ncyxie/Scriblucent/issues) with the steps to reproduce them.
