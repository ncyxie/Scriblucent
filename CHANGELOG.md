# Scriblucent releases

## 1.0.0-beta.2

### A clearer introduction. A properly branded setup.

**Write. Recite. Get it right.**

Scriblucent is a substantial reworking of the original NoteCards project: a connected Windows workspace for notes, flashcards, mind maps, and quizzes, with a redesigned interface and local storage at its core.

[![Explore the Scriblucent workspace](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ncyxie/Scriblucent/v1.0.0-beta.1/Screenshots/dashboard-dark.png)](https://github.com/ncyxie/Scriblucent#one-place-to-think-and-learn)

This update polishes the first beta's release packaging:

- **Published by ncyxie.** Windows setup and application metadata now identify the current publisher. The license identifies ncyxie's work and retains attribution to the original NoteCards code.
- **A real release overview.** The launch notes now cover the redesigned workspaces, daily workflows, and optional local AI, with a look at the app in motion.

The application features are unchanged from beta.1. Read the [full Scriblucent 1.0 beta overview](https://github.com/ncyxie/Scriblucent/releases/tag/v1.0.0-beta.1) for the transformation from NoteCards.

### Install

Choose **win-x64-Setup.exe** for most Windows PCs or **win-arm64-Setup.exe** for Windows on Arm from the assets below. Required runtimes are included. Run setup to update an existing installation; notes, study materials, settings, and models stay on your computer.

Found a problem? [Report it here](https://github.com/ncyxie/Scriblucent/issues).

### Four tools, one connected workspace

| Create | Make it useful |
| --- | --- |
| **Notes** | Write with rich text and images, link related notes, reuse templates, and return to earlier versions. |
| **Flashcards** | Build sets with text, images, and audio. Practice with spaced repetition and follow your progress. |
| **Mind maps** | Connect ideas, arrange branches, style nodes, and link them to your notes. |
| **Quizzes** | Mix choice, true/false, and written questions, then review attempts and missed answers. |

### An interface that feels alive

The dashboard and editors share a renewed visual language: light and dark themes, soft depth, cursor-responsive card tints, and coordinated motion. Tabs connect your open materials, while smooth caret movement makes writing feel more fluid. Reduced motion keeps the experience quieter when you prefer it.

### Less friction in the daily work

- **Organize your way.** Group and tag materials, search your collection, and drag cards into a manual order.
- **Work in batches.** Select multiple items for common actions, including bulk renaming and export where supported.
- **Keep practice in view.** Plan reminders in the dashboard calendar and choose the first day of your week.
- **Move between materials.** Keep editors open in tabs and connect study materials to their source notes.
- **Stay comfortable.** Choose English or Lithuanian, adjust motion, and work with large collections through on-demand card rendering.

### Local first, AI when you want it

Write, organize, and study offline without an AI model. Optional local AI can turn notes into draft flashcards, mind maps, and quizzes. Models download when you choose them and run locally afterward. Review generated material before studying it.

### Ready for Windows

Setup installers are available for **x64** and **ARM64**, with the required runtimes included. Optional models download separately. This is a beta release; [report issues](https://github.com/ncyxie/Scriblucent/issues) with the steps to reproduce them.
